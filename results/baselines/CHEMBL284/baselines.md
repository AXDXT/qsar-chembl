RMSE and MAE are pIC50 log units; R² is dimensionless.

95% percentile intervals from 2000 whole-test-scaffold bootstrap draws, compound-weighted metrics, seed 42; same draws for both models. Fixed fitted models, training sets and splits: no retraining or split-seed uncertainty. Cross-core similarity can still couple groups. Intervals are descriptive, not a significance test. Degenerate R2 draws are excluded and counted in the log.

Not estimated: train scores are resubstitution diagnostics of the fitted estimator, not an estimate of generalisation; test intervals are reported.

Stateless per-molecule cached descriptors only. No scaling, imputation, variance filtering or feature selection. Non-finite values abort. Both estimators, including the mean, fit training rows only. Equal compound weights; no weighting by repeated report count.

Declared before the first run; no search. The mean ignores chemistry and is the trivial floor. The forest uses 200 bootstrap trees to reduce Monte Carlo variation, all descriptors at each node, fully grown trees and unit minimum leaf size as a conventional untuned regression baseline. Its flexibility makes train/test gaps visible. One worker and seed 42 fix the execution. These are engineering defaults, not evidence of optimality.

Full estimator parameters (including library defaults) and input/output checksums are in baselines.json. Each predictions CSV contains both partitions with explicit labels. Saved Gate 2 assignments are read directly, never regenerated by this module.

The Gate 2 fingerprint nearest-neighbour probe remains a separate structural leakage diagnostic. These baselines supersede it as the floor Gate 4 must beat, not as the evidence for the Gate 2 evaluation contrast. No fingerprint model is fitted here.

Literature/patent-reported IC50; no efficacy, safety or suitability claim.
