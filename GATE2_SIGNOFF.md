## Repeat dispersion and its limit

The pooled within-compound SD is **0.828 pIC50 log units**, computed from 476 repeated
compounds, 1,366 reports and 890 degrees of freedom. It is RMSE-comparable in units,
but it describes individual reported measurements rather than prediction error on medians.
The formula is sqrt(sum of within-compound squared deviations from each group mean /
sum of (group size − 1)); see [`replicate_noise.json`](results/curation/CHEMBL284/replicate_noise.json).

Sensitivity subsets give **0.310** for 0.01 ≤ spread ≤ 1 and **1.011** for spread ≥0.01.
Neither is a validated bound. Reports may share provenance and differ in assay conditions;
only 12.0% of compounds have repeated reports. There are 108 groups spanning >1 log unit.
199 groups agree exactly; another 23 have small nonzero spread below 0.01. Similar values
alone do not prove independent repeats or copied records.

**Needs decision:** approve citing 0.828 as descriptive repeat dispersion, with
0.310/1.011 as sensitivity statistics, and defer a Gate 4 escape threshold until its
assumptions can be justified. This is gpt's recommendation, not Aadit's approval.
The present pull cannot establish an irreducible-error threshold for compound medians.
No automatic escape clause is signed off. Approval of this document does not start Gate 3.

Random allocation lets 582/793 test compounds reuse a training core. The largest five core
groups contain 133, 66, 60, 52 and 40 compounds, with members on both sides of the random
boundary. This lets the probe reuse analogue-series labels despite zero exact identity
overlap. CHEMBL1422's repeated reports have already become one compound, so duplicate rows
cannot supply extra fitting weight or cross the boundary. The RMSE increase is 0.159 log
units; changed test chemistry and target distributions also contribute, so this is not a
causal estimate of leakage. Intervals from 2,000 whole-test-scaffold bootstrap draws overlap
and condition on fixed training sets, predictions and splits; they are not a significance test.

Scaffold holdout blocks exact cores, including all 16 acyclic compounds as one empty core.
Singleton cores follow the same seeded allocation rule. Residual close neighbours remain;
the result measures transfer to unseen exact cores, not complete chemical independence.
Similarity clustering is deferred because it introduces a further threshold-dependent
extrapolation question; exhaustive similarity checks already expose scaffold holdout's limit.
Both test ranges lie inside their training ranges, though the scaffold test omits the
weakest extremes (test 3.000–9.921 versus train −0.041–10.097). Labels never choose the split.

## Judgement calls to defend in the viva

- Keep binding IC50 against the human target; do not infer the assay expression host.
- Refuse mass concentrations: 48 `ug.mL-1` records lack the tested material's reliable
  molecular weight. Report all 131 unit/value refusals rather than silently dropping them.
- Remove non-exact relations. Gate 1's 796 equals 791 step-5 removals plus five null-value,
  null-relation rows already refused at step 4; the 4,859 surviving records agree exactly.
- Sanitise, apply RDKit cleanup, choose the largest organic-preferred fragment, neutralise,
  canonicalise and generate InChIKey before splitting. All four structure failures are missing
  SMILES; no sanitisation failure was observed. Salt/mixture handling affects 855 records,
  neutralisation two. Largest-fragment handling assumes the selected fragment is the analyte.
- Use full standardised InChIKey for identity; retain molecule/parent IDs for audit.
  32 compounds absorb 35 extra molecule IDs. Distinct full keys stay distinct. The old
  "286 pairs" wording was wrong: 224 shared-connectivity groups contain 286 compounds beyond
  the first and 382 unordered pairs. Connectivity alone does not diagnose why keys differ.
- CHEMBL1422 has 96 raw rows, 63 accepted. With three other molecule IDs it becomes one
  compound with 71 reports, median 7.74 and spread 4.14. Collapse its weight and retain its flag.
- Keep src_id 37, BindingDB patent bioactivity: 522 accepted records versus 4,333 primary
  literature records. The saved source metadata resolves the ID; reporting regimes may differ.
- Use median pIC50; retain and flag disagreement >1 log unit rather than selecting on it.
  Retain validity comments (133 "Outside typical range", 53 "Potential transcription error")
  and weak extremes. These are review flags, not permission to optimise the held-out metric.
- Report round-number patterns and weak-tail values without inventing their causes. Two
  compounds have IC50 >10 mM. There is no visible weak-tail ceiling spike, but no recorded
  top-concentration field to rule out unlabelled censoring. Assay-source review would settle it.
- Use compound-level random and whole-core scaffold allocation, with zero-identity assertions
  and exhaustive near-neighbour reporting. Keep residual pairs in the audit and evaluation.
- Use the fixed probe only for this evaluation contrast; do not select models or tune scores.
- Pin RDKit 2026.9.1 for the verified macOS arm64 PyPI wheel-build fault in 2026.9.2, not an
  RDKit source defect. Other platforms/builds need not inherit that workaround indefinitely.
- Treat all measured repeat SDs as descriptive until independence, assay comparability and
  uncertainty of the aggregated targets can support a stronger claim.
